# Project Charter

## Problem Statement
Classify enduro mountain bikes using Azure AI to build a portfolio-grade project.

## Vision

## Commercial Considerations (To Revisit)

This project is primarily a portfolio and learning artifact designed to showcase my skills in project management and AI product development/management.  
However, limited commercial potential may exist if ownership and licensing issues are addressed.

### Potential Applications
- **Bike Park Analytics:**  
  Aggregated, anonymized bike identification could help bike parks understand what models are being ridden on their trails. This data could inform:
  - rental fleet planning  
  - parts and service inventory  
  - trail maintenance priorities  
  - seasonal demand forecasting  

- **Bike Shop Insights:**  
  Local shops could use anonymized model distribution data to guide stocking decisions for frames, components, and consumables.

- **Industry Feedback Loops:**  
  With proper permissions and ethical data handling, aggregated model trends could be sold to manufacturers to understand real‑world usage patterns.

These ideas are exploratory and outside the scope of the MVP, but they demonstrate that the underlying technology has niche commercial relevance.
